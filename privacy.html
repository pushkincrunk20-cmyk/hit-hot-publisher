Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

Current status: HIT HOT Publisher's website is an informational, static website. TikTok account connection and Content Posting API features are not active on this website yet.

1. Who operates this project?

HIT HOT Publisher is a music-content publishing project associated with the HIT HOT music community. You can contact the project through private messages to the HIT HOT VK community.

2. Information processed when visiting this website

This website does not ask visitors to register, does not contain an account login form, and does not intentionally set advertising or analytics cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub, as the hosting provider, may process technical connection information such as IP addresses, browser details, and request logs under its own terms and privacy practices.

3. Planned account integrations

Future TikTok features, if approved and launched, will require the account holder's explicit consent through TikTok's authorization flow. Depending on the granted permissions, the service may need to process basic TikTok profile identifiers, authorization tokens, draft/video metadata, and the status of uploads or posts for the sole purpose of the requested publishing functions.

No TikTok account information is collected through this static website at present. Before an account-connection feature becomes available, we will update this policy to describe the live data flows, security controls, storage, retention periods, and deletion procedures.

4. Purpose and disclosure

The intended use of any future authorized data is account connection, preparing approved video content, publishing requested content, and troubleshooting errors. We do not plan to sell personal data or share it for targeted advertising. Data may need to pass between the authorized platform, hosting providers, and systems necessary to deliver the service, with applicable permissions.

5. Security and retention

There are no user accounts or TikTok credentials stored by this static website. For future functionality, appropriate secure credential storage, access controls, and retention processes will be required before launch; they are not represented as operating today.

6. Your choices and requests

If TikTok account connection becomes available, you will be able to withdraw authorization through TikTok's account/app settings, subject to TikTok's available controls. For questions about data, access, deletion, or this policy, contact HIT HOT via private VK community messages. Please do not send passwords, API keys, or access tokens.

7. External services

Links on this website may lead to GitHub, VK, Telegram, or TikTok. Their individual privacy policies govern their own websites and services.

8. Policy updates

We may revise this Privacy Policy as the project evolves. The revision date will be shown above. Material changes should be published before new personal-data processing begins.
